# 4.16-English Technical Interview Practice Questions (with English Answers + Chinese Notes)

## Document Description
This note is designed to help you prepare for English interviews in cloud-native/ops/DevOps roles that involve a mix of English and Chinese. The goal is not to give overly advanced answers but to:
1. Provide advance practice for frequently asked questions
2. Offer direct English responses
3. Include Chinese explanations to aid understanding
4. Ensure your answers are realistic and accurate, without exaggeration

# Question 1: Please introduce yourself.

## Possible interviewer question:
Could you please introduce yourself?

## English Answer:
My name is Shiyongqiang. I have around six years of experience in operations. My main expertise lies in Linux, Kubernetes, as well as monitoring and troubleshooting. I have worked in hybrid cloud environments, handling infrastructure support, migration projects, and daily operations. Recently, my focus has been on Kubernetes, particularly application deployment and issue resolution. Currently, I am seeking a role that allows me to continue developing my skills in these areas.

## Chinese Meaning:
My name is xxx. I have about six years of experience in operations and infrastructure support. My main areas of expertise include Linux, Kubernetes, monitoring, and troubleshooting. I have worked in hybrid cloud environments and handled various tasks such as infrastructure support, migration projects, and daily operations. Recently, I have been focusing on Kubernetes, especially application deployment and problem-solving. I am now looking for a position related to Kubernetes, cloud operations, or DevOps to further enhance my practical skills.

## Why This Answer Is Effective:
1. It provides a clear and concise introduction without exaggeration.
2. It covers your key areas of experience: operations, Kubernetes, monitoring, and troubleshooting.
3. It naturally leads to discussing roles that align with these skills.
4. The focus on recent development shows your commitment to continuous learning.

## Key Terms Explanation:
- operations = operations work
- infrastructure support = infrastructure assistance
- Kubernetes = Kubernetes technology
- monitoring = system monitoring
- troubleshooting = problem-solving

# Question 2: Do you have experience with Kubernetes?

## Possible interviewer question:
Tell me about your experience with Kubernetes.

## English Answer:
I have practical experience using Kubernetes for deployment, service access, storage management, and basic troubleshooting. I am familiar with common Kubernetes objects such as Deployment, StatefulSet, Service, ConfigMap, and Ingress. Recently, I have also been practicing application deployment and troubleshooting in this context.

## Chinese Meaning:
I have hands-on experience with Kubernetes in areas like deployment, service access, storage management, and basic fault resolution. I am familiar with common Kubernetes components such as Deployment, StatefulSet, Service, ConfigMap, and Ingress. Recently, I have been focusing on application deployment and troubleshooting within Kubernetes.

## Why This Answer Is Effective:
1. It avoids claiming “expertise” to prevent overstatement.
2. It clearly lists the specific areas of experience, demonstrating practical knowledge.
3. The mention of recent practice shows your ongoing engagement with the technology.

## Key Terms Explanation:
- hands-on experience = practical application
- service access = service functionality
- storage management = storage operations
- common objects = frequently used components

# Question 3: How do you troubleshoot issues in Kubernetes?

## Possible interviewer question:
How do you address problems that arise in Kubernetes?

## English Answer:
I typically follow a step-by-step approach. First, I check the Pod status and related events. Then, I examine the logs and service connectivity. Next, I verify the configuration, resource usage, and any dependencies. If necessary, I also investigate node status and network-related issues.

## Chinese Meaning:
I usually start by checking the Pod status and related events. Then, I analyze the logs and service connectivity. Next, I confirm the configuration, resource usage, and dependencies. If required, I also look into node status and network problems.

## Why This Answer Is Effective:
1. It provides a clear and systematic approach to troubleshooting.
2. It shows your ability to think logically in problem-solving situations.
3. The use of technical terms like “Pod status” and “dependencies” demonstrates familiarity with the framework.

## Key Terms Explanation:
- step-by-step approach = systematic troubleshooting process
- Pod status = Pod operational status
- events = system notifications
- logs = diagnostic records
- service connectivity = service functionality
- dependencies = interdependent components
