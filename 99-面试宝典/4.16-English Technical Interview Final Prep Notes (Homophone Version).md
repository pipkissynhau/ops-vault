# 4.16 - English Technical Interview Final Preparation Notes (Homophones Version, for Following Along)

## Document Notes
This note is for preparing for an "English and Chinese mixed" cloud-native / operations / DevOps position English interview.

This version's goal is not to speak highly, but to:
1. Be able to speak
2. Be able to read along
3. Be able to self-rescue when not understanding
4. Not break down when being asked follow-up questions
5. Use the simplest English to explain technical concepts
6. Naturally conclude the interview at the end

> Note: The following "homophones" are **approximate pronunciation aids to help you speak**, not standard phonetic symbols. It's most important that you can read along smoothly first.

---

## One, Remember: What Will Help You Survive This Interview

You are not surviving because of "good English",  
You are surviving because of these three things:

1. Life-saving phrases
2. Keyword recognition
3. Short sentence answers

---

## Two, The Most Important Life-saving Phrases (Must Be Placed First)

### 1. Didn't Understand, Ask the Other Person to Repeat
**Sorry, could you repeat the question more slowly?**  
Homophones: **Sao Rui, Kuo De You Rui Pi Te Zhe Kua Si Chen Mo Er Si Lü?**  
👉 Sorry, could you repeat the question more slowly?

### 2. Didn't Understand, Ask the Other Person to Rephrase
**Could you rephrase the question?**  
Homophones: **Kuo De You Rui Fei Zhe Zhe Kua Si Chen?**  
👉 Could you rephrase the question?

### 3. Brain Blank, Request a Few Seconds
**Let me think for a second.**  
Homophones: **Lai Te Mi Xin Ke Fu A Sa Ken De**  
👉 Let me think for a second.

### 4. Only Heard Keywords, Confirm the Question First
**Are you asking about my role in the project?**  
Homophones: **A You Ai Si Jing A Bao Tu Mai Rong Yu Pin Jie Ke Te?**  
👉 Are you asking about my role in the project?

### 5. Only Heard Challenge, Confirm First
**Are you asking about the main challenge?**  
Homophones: **A You Ai Si Jing A Bao Tu Mai Zhen Chen?**  
👉 Are you asking about the main challenge?

### 6. Only Heard Kubernetes and Troubleshooting
**Are you asking about my troubleshooting experience in Kubernetes?**  
Homophones: **A You Ai Si Jing A Bao Tu Mai Tuo La Bu Shu Ding Yi Xi Pi Rian Su Ku Bo Nei Te Si?**  
👉 Are you asking about my troubleshooting experience in Kubernetes?

### 7. Heard Terraform, Uncertain
**Are you asking about my Terraform experience?**  
Homophones: **A You Ai Si Jing A Bao Tu Mai Tai Ruo Fu Meng Yi Xi Pi Rian Su?**  
👉 Are you asking about my Terraform experience?

### 8. Don't Know, But Can't Just Say "Don't Know"
**My hands-on experience is limited.**  
Homophones: **Mai Han Zi Ang Yi Xi Pi Rian Su Li Mi Ti De**  
👉 My hands-on experience is limited.

### 9. Understand a Bit, But Still Learning
**I understand the basic idea, and I am still learning.**  
Homophones: **Ai An De Su Dan De Bei She Ke Ai Di, An De Ai Mei Shi Li Er Lin**  
👉 I understand the basic idea, and I am still learning.

### 10. English is Average, Set Expectations First
**My spoken English is limited, but I can communicate about technical topics.**  
Homophones: **Mai Si Pao Ken Ing Gli Shi Li Mi Ti De, Ba Te Kan Ke Mu Ni Ke Ai Ti Ke Ni Ke Tu Pi Xue**  
👉 My spoken English is limited, but I can communicate about technical topics.

**It is a bit hard for me to explain in English. Can I explain this part in Chinese?**

## 17. Final Reminders

This interview is not about "saying a lot," but rather:

1. Listen for keywords  
2. Determine the question type  
3. Answer with short sentences  
4. Ask for repetition if you don't understand  
5. Be honest if you don't know  
6. Politely wrap up at the end

Your goal is not to have excellent English, but rather:

👉 To be able to simply explain technical content  
👉 To be able to start speaking  
👉 To be able to sustain the interview with short sentences and safety phrases
