# Question 1: Please Give a Self-Introduction

# Question 3: How Do You Troubleshoot Problems in Kubernetes?

## Interviewer May Ask
How do you troubleshoot problems in Kubernetes?

## English Answer
I usually follow a step-by-step approach.

First, I check the pod status and events.

Then I check logs and service connectivity.

After that, I verify configuration, resource usage, and dependencies.

If needed, I also check node status and network-related issues.

## Chinese Meaning
I usually follow a step-by-step approach to troubleshooting.

First, I check pod status and events.

Then I check logs and service connectivity.

After that, I verify configuration, resource usage, and dependencies.

If needed, I also check node status and network-related issues.

## Why Answer This Way
1. This is a very standard troubleshooting response with clear logic
2. Even if English is not perfect, this structured approach prevents confusion
3. The answer shows your troubleshooting mindset rather than just memorized concepts

## Keyword Annotations
