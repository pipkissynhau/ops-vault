## VI. Shortest Oral Version (You can memorize this)

### Shortest Troubleshooting Case Example

I once encountered a situation where both the Pod and Service seemed normal, but the business access was still failing. I started by checking the K8s resource layer, then verified the business access and the container configurations. Finally, I found that after changing the ConfigMap, the Pod had not actually been updated, so the business was still using the old configuration. After resolving this issue, I realized that release validation should not only focus on the resource status but also include business verification and confirmation that the configurations have taken effect.

### Shortest Release Case Example

I haven't fully led a release process myself, but I have participated in deployments and troubleshooting issues after releases. I've encountered situations where the configuration had been changed and the resources had been applied, but the business hadn't actually updated. Upon investigation, it turned out that although the configuration objects had changed, the running instances had not been updated consistently. This experience made me place more emphasis on release standards, such as version traceability, instance update confirmation, business verification, and backup plans. These should all be included in the standard process.
