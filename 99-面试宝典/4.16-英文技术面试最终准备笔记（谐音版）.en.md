# 4.16 - Final Notes for English Technical Interviews (Homophonic Version for Easy Reading Aloud)

## Document Description
These notes are designed to help you prepare for English interviews in cloud-native, operations, or DevOps roles that involve a mix of Chinese and English.

The goal of this version is not to use advanced language, but rather to ensure that:
1. You can start speaking.
2. You can read the prepared content aloud.
3. You know how to handle situations where you don’t understand.
4. You remain calm when asked follow-up questions.
5. You can explain technical concepts in simple English.
6. You can end the interview smoothly.

> Note: The “homophonic” versions below are approximate pronunciations designed to help you start speaking, not standard phonetic transcriptions. It’s most important that you can read them aloud smoothly first.

---

## I. First and Foremost: What Will Help You Survive This Interview

You won’t survive this interview by being “extremely fluent in English.” Instead, it’s about these three things:
1. Life-saving phrases.
2. Ability to identify key words.
3. Short-answer techniques.

---

## II. The Most Important Life-Saving Phrases (Place These at the Beginning)

### 1. If I Don’t Understand, Please Repeat It
**Sorry, could you repeat the question more slowly?**  
Homophonic version: **Sao Rui, Ku De You Rui Pi Te He Kuai Si Chen Mo Er Si Lou Li?**  
👉 Sorry, can you say it more slowly?

### 2. If I Don’t Understand, Please Rephrase It
**Could you rephrase the question?**  
Homophonic version: **Ku De You Rui Fre Zi He Kuai Si Chen?**  
👉 Can you put it another way?

### 3. If My Brain Goes Blank, Give Me a Few Seconds
**Let me think for a second.**  
Homophonic version: **Lait Mi Xin Ke Fo A Sai Ken De.**  
👉 Let me think for a moment.

### 4. If I Only Heard Key Words, Confirm the Question First
**Are you asking about my role in the project?**  
Homophonic version：**A You Ai Si Jing Ah Bao Te Mai Rou Ou Yin He Kuai Pu Jue Ke Te?**  
👉 Are you referring to my role in this project?

### 5. If I Only Heard “challenge”, Confirm First
**Are you asking about the main challenge?**  
Homophonic version：**A You Ai Si Jing Ah Bao Te He Mei Yin Cha Leng Zhi?**  
👉 Are you talking about the main challenge?

### 6. If I Heard “Kubernetes” and “troubleshooting”, Confirm
**Are you asking about my troubleshooting experience in Kubernetes?**  
Homophonic version：**A You Ai Si Jing Ah Bao Te Mai Te La Bu Shu Ding Yi克斯皮瑞恩斯 Yin Ku Bo Ne Ti Si?**  
👉 Are you referring to my troubleshooting experience with Kubernetes?

### 7. If I Heard “Terraform” and’m Not Sure
**Are you asking about my Terraform experience?**  
Homophonic version：**A You Ai Si Jing Ah Bao Te Mai Tai Ruo Fu Mu Yi克斯皮瑞恩斯?**  
👉 Are you asking about my experience with Terraform?

### 8. If I Don’t Know, But Can’t Just Say “I Don’t Know”
**My hands-on experience is limited.**  
Homophonic version：**Mai Han Zi Ang Yi克斯皮瑞恩斯 Yi Zi Li Mi Ti De.**  
👉 My practical experience is limited.

### 9. I Understand Some Basics, but Am Still Learning
**I understand the basic idea, and I am still learning.**  
Homophonic version：**Ai Ans Dan De He Kuai Pei Se Ke Ai Di E, And Er Ai Ai Em Steil Le Ning.**  
👉 I grasp the basic concepts, but I’m still learning.

### 10. If My English Is Average, Set Expectations First
**My spoken English is limited, but I can communicate about technical topics.**  
Homophonic version：**Mai Si Poun Ken Ingliʃ Yi Zi Li Mi Ti De, Ba Te Ai Kan Ke Miu Ni Kaite Ah Bao Te Tai Koni Kou To Pi克斯.**  
👉 My English is not excellent, but I can discuss technical matters.

**It is a bit hard for me to explain in English. Can I explain this part in Chinese?**  
Homophonic version： **Yi Te Yi Zi Ah Bi Te Ha De Fo Mi Tu Yi克斯普雷因 Yin Ingliʃ. Kan Ai Yi克斯普雷인 Di Si Pat Yin Pa Tin Si?**  
👉👉 If necessary, I will also inspect the nodes and the network.👉 I am interested in this position because it is related to Kubernetes and DevOps.
