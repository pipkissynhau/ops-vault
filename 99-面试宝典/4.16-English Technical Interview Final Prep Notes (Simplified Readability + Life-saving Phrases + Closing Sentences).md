# 4.16 - Final Preparation Notes for English Technical Interviews (Simplified Readability + Life-Saving Phrases + Closing Sentences)

## Document Explanation
This note is for preparing for "semi-English and semi-Chinese" interviews for cloud-native / operations / DevOps positions.

This version's goal is not to sound advanced, but:
1. To be able to speak
2. To self-rescue when not understanding
3. To stay calm when being pressed
4. To explain technical concepts with the simplest English
5. To naturally conclude the interview at the end

---

## One: Remember - What Will Help You Survive This Interview

You are not surviving because of "good English",  
You are surviving because of these three things:

1. Life-Saving Phrases
2. Keyword Recognition
3. Short Answers

## Seventeen, Final Reminder

This interview isn't about "speaking a lot," but rather:

1. Listen for keywords  
2. Judge the question type  
3. Answer with short sentences  
4. Ask for repetition if you don't understand  
5. Be honest if you don't know  
6. Politely wrap up at the end

Your goal isn't to have great English, but rather:  
👉 To be able to simply explain technical content
