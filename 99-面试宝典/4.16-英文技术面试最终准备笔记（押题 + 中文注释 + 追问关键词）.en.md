# 4.16-Final Preparation Notes for English Technical Interviews (Predictive Questions + Chinese Annotations + Key Follow-up Terms)

## Document Description
This note is designed to help with interviews for cloud-native/ops/DevOps positions that involve a mix of English and Chinese.

The goal isn't to speak in an extremely advanced way, but rather to:
- Understand the questions
- Express technical concepts in simple English
- Handle situations where you don't understand
- Stay calm during follow-up inquiries
- Avoid exaggeration

---

## I. Core Interview Strategies (Must Remember)

Your approach should be:

👉 Be truthful about your technical skills + Use brief sentences + Ask for clarification if needed + Admit when you don't know

You're not there to show off your English; you're there to:

👉 Convey 6 years of experience in 10 sentences

---

## II. Opening Phrases (Recommended to Memorize)

My spoken English is limited, but I can communicate about technical topics.  
👉 My English is not perfect, but I can discuss technical matters.

If I miss anything, I may ask you to repeat or rephrase the question.  
👉 If I don't hear clearly, I'll ask you to repeat or reword your question.

# VII. 🔥 Key Follow-up Terms (Most Important)

This is the most critical part of the interview!

---

## 1️⃣ Asking about Responsibilities (Essential)
Keywords:
- role
- responsibility
- part

👉 Meaning: What specific tasks were you responsible for?

Answer:
My role was mainly infrastructure preparation and environment setup.  
► My main responsibilities included preparing the infrastructure and setting up the environment.

---

## 2️⃣ Asking about Challenges (Essential)
Keywords:
- challenge
- issue
- problem

👉 Meaning: What difficulties did you encounter?

Answer:
One main challenge was network connectivity.  
► One significant problem was network connectivity.

---

## 3️⃣ Asking how You Solved It (Essential)
Keywords:
- solve
- handle
- deal with

👉 Meaning: How did you address these issues?

Answer:
We checked it step by step and verified connectivity.  
► We systematically investigated and confirmed the connectivity.

---

## 4️⃣ Asking about the Result (Essential)
Keywords:
- result
- successful

👉 Meaning: What was the outcome?

Answer:
The migration was completed successfully.  
► The migration was successfully finished.

---

## 5️⃣ Asking if You Led the Project (Frequent)
Keywords:
- lead
- independently

👉 Meaning: Did you take the lead on this project?

Answer:
I did not lead it independently, but I was responsible for important parts.  
► I didn't lead it entirely, but I handled key aspects.
